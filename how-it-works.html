How it works

Up and running in
days, not months.

No new apps. No logins. No technical setup. Just WhatsApp — which your club already uses every day.

Setup process

Three steps to a
smarter patrol.

01
We set it up
Once your club signs up, we configure a dedicated WhatsApp number and load your club's documents — patrol rosters, SOPs, first aid procedures, equipment lists, training schedules, and FAQs. This takes a few days, not weeks. You don't need to change anything about how your club runs.
02
Members message in
Patrol captains, vice captains, and members message your club's WhatsApp number with questions. No registration, no passwords, no training. ClubBot reads the docs you've already written and answers instantly. When it can't answer, it tells you who to ask next.
03
You keep it current
When your roster changes or a procedure updates, your admin drops a file in a shared folder — Google Drive, Dropbox, or wherever you keep things. ClubBot picks it up automatically. No IT request, no restart, no fuss.
The tech behind it

Under the bonnet.

Claude AI — ClubBot uses Anthropic's Claude (the same model trusted by enterprises) to understand questions and find the right answers in your documents. It's fast, accurate, and gets better as your docs grow.
Your own knowledge base — ClubBot works from your club's actual documents. We don't train on your data, we don't share your conversations, and we don't pull answers from the internet. What your club knows is what ClubBot knows.
Real-time data — Surf conditions, tides, and weather come from official Australian Bureau of Meteorology and Manly Hydraulics Lab sources. Shark alerts come from the NSW Shark Management Program. All calibrated to your beach.

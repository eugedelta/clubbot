Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 26 October 2026

ClubBot is a WhatsApp assistant for surf lifesaving clubs. This policy explains what information we collect when you use ClubBot, why we collect it, and how it's handled. We've written it in plain English because we think you deserve to understand it without a law degree.

01

Who we are

ClubBot is operated by Delta Work Pty Ltd (ACN 697 948 119, ABN 89 697 948 119), based in Sydney, Australia. When you use ClubBot, you're using a service Delta Work builds and runs on behalf of your surf club.

If you have questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact us at gday@clubbot.com.au.

02

What we collect

When you message a ClubBot number, we collect and store the following:

DataWhy we collect itHow long we keep it
Your WhatsApp phone number To identify you and send replies back to you For as long as you use ClubBot at your club, or until you ask us to delete it
Your patrol team (if you tell us) To answer questions about your roster and patrols For as long as you use ClubBot at your club, or until you ask us to delete it
Your messages and our replies To answer you. ClubBot uses your last 5 exchanges to understand follow-up questions 12 months, then deleted
Your SLS member number (if you give it) To check your club membership and tailor answers For as long as you use ClubBot at your club, or until you ask us to delete it
Feedback responses To measure and improve answer quality 12 months, then deleted
Usage logs (message type, model used, token counts) To run the service and track costs 12 months, then deleted

If your club's agreement with us sets a shorter period, we follow that instead. For example, a pilot agreement may require us to delete your data within 30 days after the pilot ends.

We don't collect your name, email address, location, age or date of birth, or any other personal information unless your club adds it to your member profile on its behalf.

Please don't send ClubBot health details, incident reports or other people's personal information. It doesn't need them to answer your questions.

03

How your messages are processed

This is the most important section to understand. When you send a message to ClubBot, it takes this path:

Your phone → Meta (WhatsApp) → ClubBot servers (Railway) → Anthropic API → back to you.
Three companies handle your messages on our behalf. Each has its own privacy policy.

Meta (WhatsApp): Your messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, under Meta's WhatsApp Privacy Policy and Business Tools Terms.

Anthropic: Your message is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the answer. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models. It deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days except in the limited cases set out in Anthropic's privacy policy.

Railway: Our servers and database are hosted by Railway, whose servers are in the United States.

Meta, Anthropic and Railway may store or process your data outside Australia, including in the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure it is handled securely and in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Your club's documents (handbooks, rosters, procedures) may be held in Google Drive. Your messages and phone number are not.

04

Who can see your data

Access to your data is limited to the following:

Your club can see that you are registered with its ClubBot. It also receives usage reports: how many members use ClubBot, the questions asked most often, and questions ClubBot couldn't answer. Reports show only the last 4 digits of phone numbers, never your full number. Your club cannot browse your conversations.

Delta Work (us) can see conversations, usage logs and quality measures, to run and improve the service. We review conversations to fix wrong or missing answers and check answer quality, including with an automated daily review. Only Delta Work does this.

No one else. We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your data to anyone for commercial purposes. We only disclose it if Australian law requires us to.

05

What we don't do

To be clear:

We do not sell your data to advertisers or anyone else.

We do not use your messages to train AI models.

We do not share your WhatsApp number or SLS member number with other clubs or organisations.

We do not send you unsolicited messages. ClubBot only replies when you message it first.

06

Your rights

Under Australian privacy law, you can:

Ask for the personal information we hold about you.

Ask us to correct anything that's wrong.

Ask us to delete your data. We delete your member record, message logs and conversation history within 30 days of a verified request. You'll need to register again if you want to use ClubBot later.

Opt out of feedback requests at any time.

To do any of these, email gday@clubbot.com.au. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

07

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit, access controls on our database, and secure storage of passwords and API keys.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell your club and the members affected as soon as we can, and notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where the law requires it.

If you notice a security concern relating to ClubBot, please email gday@clubbot.com.au straight away.

08

Children and Cadets

ClubBot is for surf club patrol members aged 15 and over. A club may also offer ClubBot to its Cadets aged 13 and 14, if the club has told their parents or guardians and they have agreed.

We handle Cadets' data in exactly the same way as everyone else's. We don't collect anyone's age or date of birth, so we rely on the club to decide who is invited.

The Nippers persona, where a club has it, is for parents and Nippers coordinators, not for children to use themselves.

If you think a child has started using ClubBot without a parent's or guardian's agreement, email gday@clubbot.com.au and we'll delete their data promptly. A parent or guardian can also ask us to delete their child's data at any time.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the dates at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll tell clubs directly so they can let their members know. If you keep using ClubBot after an update, the updated policy applies.

Questions about your privacy?

We're happy to explain anything in this policy or talk through how your data is handled.
